# 3DHV
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
Explore computer hardware by rotating three-dimensional models and selecting numbered components to reveal what they do.

## Users
Assumption based on the existing lesson content: students and people learning PC hardware.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Next.js with React Three Fiber. Motherboard, PC tower, HDD, and SATA SSD lessons. The user requests detailed front and back tower panels with buttons and ports, a motherboard built from a real web image, animated component selection, a less congested site, and a distinctive custom logo.

The separate `/test` page provides 64 authored questions across eight hardware topics, three difficulty levels and six formats. Practice reveals explanations immediately; assessment reveals them with final results. Learners can choose topics and session length, see exact-match topic scores, and retry missed questions. Sessions are held in memory, not persisted. This broad learning bank does not certify complete professional competence.

A shared left sidebar links the explorer and test page. Troubleshoot and Pioneers & creators are disabled, locked future features. The latter is reserved for contributor histories; no contributor page content has been created.

## Brand Commitments
Keep the 3DHV name. The user wants interaction inspiration from websites beyond hardware products. No specific reference site was supplied; research and implementation choices are delegated.

## Product Principles
The model leads. Selection should visibly move the view and explain the selected component. Additional learning content should appear progressively.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing lesson text and procedural HDD/SSD geometry. Official ASUS ProArt motherboard image downloaded to public/references/proart-motherboard.png. This is a photographic reference, not a manufacturer CAD model.
